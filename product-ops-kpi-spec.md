# Product Operations KPI Specification

## Purpose

Define the metrics needed to monitor product delivery, release health, data quality, and AI workflow quality.

## KPI Groups

| Group | Metric | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| Delivery | Milestone hit rate | Shows plan reliability |
| Delivery | Blocked work age | Shows whether blockers are handled quickly |
| Quality | Open critical defects | Shows release risk |
| Data | Source ingestion success rate | Shows data reliability |
| Data | Entity error rate | Shows trust risk |
| AI | Classification pass rate | Shows AI workflow quality |
| AI | Critical AI failure count | Shows safety and trust risk |
| Product | Alpha user feedback themes | Shows product-market learning |
| Operations | Incident count and resolution time | Shows production health |

## Dashboard Questions

1. Are we delivering according to plan?
2. Are we building the right scope?
3. Are releases safe?
4. Can users trust the data?
5. Are AI workflows improving quality or creating risk?
